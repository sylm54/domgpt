## What is App?

App is your personal conditioning and self-improvement coach. It uses AI to create personalized experiences that help you reach your goals through hypnosis, daily challenges, coaching conversations, and reflective exercises.

Think of it as a smart assistant that learns about you, understands your goals, and creates custom audio sessions and activities to help you transform your habits, mindset, and behaviors.

## Getting Started

### First Launch

When you first open App, you'll go through a simple onboarding process:

1. **Choose Your Style**
   - Set Coach style (soft, motivational, direct, etc.)
   - Select Hypnosis style (authoritative, permissive, balanced)

2. **Talk to Your Coach**
   - Have a natural conversation about your goals
   - Share what you want to improve (confidence, focus, habits, etc.)
   - Discuss any challenges or obstacles you face

## Core Features
### 1. Hypnosis Sessions

**What it does:** Creates personalized audio sessions designed to help you achieve specific goals through guided hypnosis.

**How it works:**
- A Hypno agent analyzes your goals and preferences
- It generates a custom hypnosis script just for you
- The script includes relaxation techniques, suggestions, and imagery
- The app converts it to high-quality audio with natural voices
- You listen and experience deep relaxation and positive change

**Session Components:**
1. **Pre-talk** (optional) - Sets expectations and builds rapport
2. **Induction** - Guides you into a relaxed, focused state
3. **Deepening** - Takes you to a deeper level of relaxation
4. **Suggestions** - Delivers messages aligned with your goals
5. **Emerging** - Gently brings you back to full awareness
6. **Post-hypnotic Suggestions** - Reinforces changes after the session

**Audio Features:**
- Multiple voice options (male and female)
- Adjustable speed and volume
- Sound effects and ambient sounds
- Special effects like echo and binaural beats
- Layered audio for enhanced depth

**When to Use:**
- Daily or several times per week
- When you have 10-30 minutes of quiet time

**Customize Your Experience:**
- **Style:** Choose how direct the suggestions should be
  - Authoritative: Clear, direct guidance
  - Permissive: Gentle, inviting approach
  - Balanced: Mix of both

- **Induction Type:** How you enter the hypnotic state
  - Progressive Relaxation: Body scanning and muscle relaxation
  - Visualization: Imagine peaceful scenes
  - Breathing: Focus on your breath

- **Sensory Focus:** Which senses the session emphasizes
  - Visual: Strong imagery and visualization
  - Kinesthetic: Focus on feelings and sensations
  - Mixed: Balanced use of all senses

- **Suggestion Style:** How suggestions are delivered
  - Direct: Clear, explicit statements
  - Indirect: Woven into stories and metaphors
  - Permissive: Presented as options and possibilities

### 2. AI Coach Chat

**What it does:** Provides ongoing guidance, answers questions, and adjusts your conditioning plan based on your progress.

**How it works:**
- Chat naturally with your AI coach about your journey
- Get personalized advice and support
- Review your progress together
- Adjust your goals and approach as needed
- Receive encouragement and motivation

**What You Can Discuss:**
- Your experiences with hypnosis and challenges
- New goals you want to add
- Obstacles or difficulties you're facing
- Insights or breakthroughs you've had
- Questions about the conditioning process
- Requests to modify your plan

**Coach Personality:**
Your coach can have different personality traits that affect how it interacts with you:

- **Soft:** Gentle and understanding
- **Motivational:** Inspiring and uplifting
- **Encouraging:** Friendly and supportive
- **Empathetic:** Shows deep understanding of your feelings
- **Direct:** Straightforward and to the point
- **Informative:** Provides detailed explanations
- **Intense:** Pushes you harder (if you want that)
- **Pushing:** Expands on your goals, takes them further
- **Assertive:** Takes initiative and guides your journey

**When to Chat with Your Coach:**
- Weekly check-ins
- When you feel stuck or unmotivated
- After completing a major challenge
- When you want to set new goals
- Whenever you need support or guidance

### 3. Daily Challenges

**What it does:** Gives you specific, real-world tasks that help you practice and reinforce your conditioning goals.

**How it works:**
- The app generates 5-8 small, actionable challenges
- Each challenge is designed to be completed in a single interaction
- Challenges are realistic and appropriate for your level
- They gradually increase in difficulty as you progress
- Complete them in your daily life and mark them as done

**Types of Challenges:**
- Social interaction tasks
- Confidence-building exercises
- Mindfulness practices
- Habit reinforcement activities
- Behavioral experiments

**Examples:**
- "Make eye contact for 3 seconds with a stranger"
- "Take 5 deep breaths before making a decision"
- "Give someone a genuine compliment"
- "Stand tall with good posture for 1 minute"
- "Speak clearly and confidently in a conversation"

**Challenge Benefits:**
- Apply what you learn in hypnosis to real life
- Build confidence through small wins
- Create new neural pathways through practice
- Track your progress over time
- Stay engaged with your goals daily

**Daily Challenge Routine:**
1. Open the Challenges page each morning
2. Review the available challenges
3. Choose one or more to complete today
4. Go about your day and complete the tasks
5. Return to the app and mark completed challenges

### 4. Reflection Sessions

**What it does:** Helps you assess your progress, gather insights, and provide feedback to improve future sessions.

**How it works:**
- The Interview Agent generates 4-8 thoughtful questions
- Questions are tailored to your recent activities and goals
- Different question formats: ratings, choices, and open-ended responses
- Your answers help track progress and personalize future content

**Types of Questions:**

**Multiple Choice:**
- "How relaxed did you feel during the session?"
- Options: Very relaxed, Somewhat relaxed, Neutral, Not very relaxed

**Rating (1-10 Scale):**
- "Rate the effectiveness of today's hypnosis session"
- "How confident do you feel after completing this challenge?"
- Scale from 1 (not at all) to 10 (completely)

**Open Text:**
- "What was your most vivid visualization during the session?"
- "What challenges did you face today?"
- "What insights or breakthroughs did you have?"

**When to Reflect:**
- After completing a hypnosis session
- After finishing daily challenges
- Weekly check-ins
- When you feel stuck or unsure of progress
- Whenever you want to journal your experience

**Benefits of Reflection:**
- Track your progress over time
- Identify what's working and what isn't
- Provide feedback to improve future sessions
- Deepen your self-awareness
- Reinforce positive changes

### 5. Dashboard

**What it does:** Your central hub for tracking progress and accessing all features.

**What You'll See:**

**Progress Statistics:**
- **Total Sessions:** How many hypnosis sessions you've completed
- **Reflections:** Number of reflection sessions completed
- **Current Streak:** Consecutive days of activity
- **Last Activity:** How long ago you last used the app

**Quick Actions:**
- **Hypnosis Session:** Play your latest session or generate a new one
- **Chat with Coach:** Start a conversation with your AI coach
- **Daily Challenges:** View and manage your challenges
- **Reflection:** Start a reflection session
- **Settings:** Configure your preferences

**Your Conditioning Plan:**
- See the current focus of your conditioning
- Understand what you're working toward
- Review instructions from your coach

**Journey History:**
- Timeline of all your activities
- See past sessions and reflections
- Review your progress over time
